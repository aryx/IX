tiny-kernel on tiny-machine, in OCaml compiled to JavaScript. What you type
goes to the console above, a line at a time; with the mouse in the screen,
to the screen's programs, key by key. Try ls, then
tiny-windows: in the screen, the right button gives a menu (New,
then a rectangle swept with the left button, and a shell is in it);
tetris typed there plays with the arrows.
