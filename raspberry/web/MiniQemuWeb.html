A Raspberry Pi 1, in OCaml compiled to JavaScript, booting mini-9pi
from its SD card. Just type: the keys go to the board's serial line,
the text above. Click the screen and they are its USB keyboard's, the
mouse its USB mouse's (Esc gives the pointer back; click outside the
screen to type on the serial line again). Try ls /bin,
then rio: in the screen, the right button gives a menu.
