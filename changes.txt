* 0.3 (Q4.2 2026) (in progress) (a desktop, C and Datalog on .NET, mini-analyze on both roads)

** dotnet
- mini-cc -dotnet, a managed C; mini-datalog -dotnet, the rules
  compiled to IL (Souffle's split); several assemblies in mini-dotnet
- mini-ml -dotnet: formats as values (F#'s way), its benchmarks
  native, by Microsoft's dotnet and by mini-dotnet; a C module (mini-cc
  -dotnet) called from ML by externals
- mini-analyze runs on .NET, its two roads (C and ML) with their
  reports; mini-dotnet's JIT starts in 110 ms where 437

** analyses
- mini-analyze -caps: a function's capabilities, OCaml's check of
  them again in Datalog; -dead and -caps over 42 of 44 programs (283
  functions dead in their program)
- pointer analyses that keep apart what OCaml's types say cannot meet;
  lib_code/datalog/FACTS.md, every fact the front ends say

** windows
- a desktop over mini-rio (windows/desktop/): rio scripted and in
  layers, panel, startmenu, desk (wallpaper and icons), settings,
  expose (the draw device's 'Z', an image k times smaller)
- idle=wait: mini-files, mini-office, mini-page asleep until used;
  mini-stats shows the memory, the disk and the network too

** misc
- Bignum's limbs of 28 bits on arm: X25519, ECDSA and RSA 54% fewer
  instructions there
- make's test targets named by category

* 0.2 (Q4.1 2026) (mini-9pi built by ix and on real Pi 1 and Pi 2 boards, mini-rio, a browser, an office, sound, IX in a web page, Doom and Quake, a PIM, a debugger, dotnet, ix built in its own system)

** kernels
- mini-xv6 and mini-9pi built by ix's tools alone (their mkfiles:
  mini-ml in ocaml-light's place, mini-cc in gcc's, mini-asm and
  mini-ld in GNU's as and ld), on the Pi 4 and the Pi 1 (mini-mk O=5),
  the Makefiles' checks passed; kernels/steps/ too; the Pi 1's kernel
  by ix's tools by default
- mini-9pi boots on a real Pi 1 (the author's model B): the card, the
  keyboard and mouse, rio, the games, the sound; overclocked to 800 MHz
- mini-9pi on the Pi 2, under QEMU and on the author's board, one card
  for both Pis; the Pi 2's four cores under one kernel lock
  (mini-stats, /dev/sysstat); the Pi 4B begun under QEMU
- mini-9pi runs ix's own programs, not principia's: a Plan 9 target
  for mini-ml (mini-mk O=5 OS=plan9, Unix over Plan 9's system calls,
  threads), mini-rc the boot shell, an SD card made by mini-mkcard
  (FAT and an xv6 partition as the root, the sources in /src)
- mini-9pi builds ix on its card, each file Linux's byte for byte
  (176 minutes on the Pi 2 under QEMU)
- mini-9pi's drivers as programs and in the kernel, the same code:
  mini-usbd and Kusb (hubs, unplug and plug), mini-dossrv and Kdos
  (FAT read and written), mini-fdisk; lib_memdraw and lib_memlayer
- mini-9pi: /dev/audio (the Pi 1's PWM and DMA), UDP, a clock, the
  compose key, the keys held (/dev/kbd), 1024 by 768, 352 MB for the
  processes, a process ended when no page is left, free
- mini-9pi: fork's copy on write, segments, #p for a debugger, a USB
  mouse's wheel, letters smoothed, a picture scaled k times by the
  draw device, the Pi's temperature and clock, a watchdog
- kernels/lib_machine/: the machine under every kernel here
- the Pi 4's kernels by OCaml 4.14 too, a third compiler; strings not
  written, Bytes for those that are
- new mini-oberon: Oberon's viewers, texts and commands on the Pi
- new mini-singularity: processes in the kernel's address space,
  channels with contracts, an exchange heap, manifests (mini-singml)
- new mini-squeak: the Blue Book's Smalltalk on the bare Pi 4, started
  from an image; also in a window on Linux and of mini-rio's

** windows
- new mini-rio (windows/, as xix's): windows swept, moved, resized,
  hidden, by the menu or the border; scrolling, selection, snarf and
  paste, UTF-8; wctl, label, cursor, /dev/screen; a thread a window
- lib_graphics (the draw device's client: Display, Draw, Font, Mouse,
  Keyboard, Menu), lib_networking/9p, lib_core/concurrency
- the draw platform draws only what changed (Redraw)

** utilities
- Plan 9's commands, as principia's and checked against them under
  mini-5i: ls, cat, echo, bind, mount, pwd, mkdir, rm, cp, mv, touch,
  chmod, sleep, seq, du, date, grep, tail, xd, uniq, tr, sed, sort,
  ps, time, test, xargs, walk, cal; each access by a capability
- new mini-awk, mini-hoc, mini-dc and mini-bc
- new mini-tar, mini-gzip and mini-gunzip
- common(1): -v, -debug, -quiet, for every program

** typesetting
- new mini-troff and mini-man: a manual page for a terminal, PDF, ms's
  papers with tables and pictures, simple LaTeX and noweb (principia's
  books typeset), a format of ix's own (Markdown and \name{text})
- ix's manual: a page for each program, how it differs from Plan 9's

** apps
- new mini-office (the playground's TinyOffice): documents with
  pictures (PNG, JPEG), exported to PDF
- new mini-page: a PDF viewer; lib_graphics's images and fonts; pages
  one under another, links followed, 60 frames a second on mini-9pi
- new mini-play (.mod, .mid, .mp3) and mini-rebirth; lib_audio;
  mini-play plays films too (FLI, AVI, MPEG-1 with its MP2)
- new mini-pim: mail (SMTP, IMAP, mini-maild, Gmail read), calendar
  with time zones, people, notes, tasks, money with envelopes, Today,
  one search over all, reminders; lib_networking/mail
- new mini-files, a file manager (icons, dragging), and mini-open
  (lib_mime: a file's kind by its bytes, the program for it)
- new mini-maps: OpenStreetMap's tiles, places, the weather, a route
  by A*
- new mini-prof (where a program's instructions go) and mini-stats

** browsers and networking
- new mini-netscape, after mini-chrome: CSS, a layout engine with
  grid, pictures (PNG, JPEG, GIF, SVG), PDF, JavaScript, cookies
- mini-netscape: tabs, history, bookmarks, helper programs (PDF to
  mini-page, films to mini-play), fields edited, mailto:; on mini-9pi
  (Hacker News in 23 s); browsers/ 23,038 lines to 19,277 (degrade,
  don't fail: no ES modules, shadow trees, Proxy; strings as UTF-8)
- new mini-lynx, mini-curl, mini-httpd, mini-node
- lib_networking: TCP, DNS, TLS 1.3 and 1.2, HTTP; lib_crypto for it
- mini-curl and mini-lynx on mini-9pi, https:// from the Internet

** editors
- new mini-emacs, after efuns: buffers, windows, the minibuffer,
  searches, dired, macros, colors by mini-ml's parser (lib_code); in a
  terminal, a window on Linux and on mini-9pi
- new mini-turbopascal: Turbo Pascal's IDE (lib_terminal)
- new mini-drscheme, with the playground's gui (lib_gui, the 7GUIs)

** games
- the author's playground in ix (lib_playground, lib_physics): Tetris,
  TinyWolfenstein, TinyCameltry, TinyMissileCommand, with sound, on
  Linux (SDL) and on mini-9pi through the draw device; TinyBreakout,
  TinyTronscroll over the network (UDP); juice, physics, ai and
  netcode in lib_playground
- new mini-doom: id's renderer, Freedoom's levels, sound, music,
  weapons, monsters; 15 frames a second on mini-9pi
- new mini-quake: Quake's software renderer, lightmaps (mini-light),
  monsters, weapons, particles, on LibreQuake's levels
- new mini-virtuaracing: three courses, the sound, the painter's
  algorithm and the PlayStation's ordering table beside the depth

** debuggers
- new mini-acid: Plan 9's acid, over mini-5i's /proc, mini-9pi's #p
  and Linux's ptrace; breakpoints by line, C and ML values by their
  types, DWARF's lines and types for gcc's programs
- mini-ld -g (5l's symbol and pc/line tables), mini-ml's and mini-cc's
  lines; lib_executable (ELF too), mini-nm, mini-where
- the stack named at a fault, an uncaught exception, mini-9pi's panic:
  the frames, the raise's line, its arguments

** dotnet
- new mini-dotnet: .NET's assemblies read and run (Microsoft's too),
  a verifier, a JIT for arm64 and arm (generics, exceptions, structs,
  a register cache, tiers); on mini-9pi's card
- new mini-tiger and mini-csc (a reduced C#), compiled to .NET
- mini-ml -dotnet: ML to IL (closures, generics, tail calls, several
  units), lib_core's units compiled to .NET

** languages
- new mini-scheme (and Landin's SECD), mini-pascal, mini-smalltalk,
  mini-forth
- new mini-prolog (and Warren's machine, -wam) and mini-datalog;
  mini-cc -facts and mini-ml -facts, -flow: pointer, control flow and
  liveness analyses of ix's own C and OCaml as Datalog rules
- new mini-analyze (analyzers/): those analyses over ix's programs, a
  bug finder, -dead, the call graph against real runs (lib_code/datalog)
- mini-datalog faster: two engines (hashed, Souffle's B-trees), -j n
  on several domains
- mini-ml's runtime: C calls ML (callback), a value stack per
  process, threads, a signal between two system calls handled; in
  parts, with ocaml-light's header names; a generational collector
- mini-ml faster: the ssa back end, -O3 (ix built with it), floats
  unboxed, ints untagged, inlining across units, CSE, loop invariants,
  jump tables; checked annotations ([@@tailrec], [@@zero_alloc]...)
- mini-cc -O3: a registerizer and ssa/, the C library and the kernels

- mini-ml: let*, bytes a type of its own; Marshal in ML; the stdlib a

- mlpp: type t = [%mli], [%list] comprehensions, |!, type classes

** generators

** toolchain
- mini-ld -H0 -T: an image without a header, at an address
- mini-asm and mini-ld: arm64's system instructions (MRS, MSR, ERET,

** machines

* 0.1 (Q3.3 2026) (first commit, m-ix and t-ix, toolchain v0, mini-qemu boots xv6 and 9pi, mini-xv6, mini-9pi, mini-ml, ix built by ix)

** general
- first commit (2026-09-21): the programs of the Principia Softwarica
  books written again in OCaml, small enough to be read, with Claude
  Code (docs/history.md, docs/yoann_notes/prompt-history.md)
- two sizes: m-ix, the mini-xxx programs, faithful twins of the
  originals and checked against them; t-ix, the tiny-xxx programs, one
  file each and free in their design (tiny/)
- less than two weeks later: 80,767 lines for m-ix with its libraries,
  15,964 for t-ix (docs/loc.md)

** milestone: ix built by ix
- ix compiles most of ix: mini-mk runs the mkfiles, mini-ml, mini-lex
  and mini-yacc compile the OCaml, mini-cc and mini-asm the C library
  and the runtime, mini-ar and mini-ld link. No INRIA OCaml, gcc,
  binutils, glibc or goken in the build
- the fixed point (mkfiles/fixpoint.sh): ix built by the programs of
  its own build gives the same 262 files, byte for byte
- eleven programs so far, arm64 only; not yet mini-git, the machines,
  the kernels, tiny/

** kernels
- new mini-xv6: xv6 in OCaml (on ocaml-light's arm back end), running
  xv6's own programs on the Pi 1 and the Pi 4
- new mini-9pi: Plan 9's Pi kernel in OCaml: boots principia's SD
  card to rc, then USB, the draw device and rio, the network (TCP); on
  the Pi 1 and the Pi 4

** machines
- new mini-5i: arm32 and arm64 user programs, with Linux's or Plan 9's
  system calls; over 30 MIPS
- new mini-qemu: a Pi 1 and a Pi 4 as QEMU's (MMU, interrupts, timer,
  UART, SD card, framebuffer, USB keyboard, mouse and network, four
  cores); boots xv6's Pi ports, principia's 9pi with rio, and ix's
  kernels
- mini-pi, to boot a kernel by its name

** toolchain
- new mini-asm and mini-ld for arm and arm64: the same executables as
  5l's and 7l's byte for byte; a.out, ELF and Mach-O
- new mini-cc: the same instructions as 5c -O0's and 7c -O0's
  (compat/), a simpler stack machine (simple/) and its passes (opti/)
- new mini-ar

** languages
- new mini-ml: a native OCaml compiler for arm64 and arm (parser,
  Scope, a Hindley-Milner checker, a stack machine; optional GNU
  assembly, passes and SSA), its runtime in one file of C
- mini-ml compiles all of ix (260 files of 260): labels, inline
  records, type-directed constructors and fields, floats, int32 and
  int64, formats, Marshal, signals, and -pp ([%bits], deriving)
- ix rewritten to simpler OCaml where that cost fewer lines than a
  feature in mini-ml: no optional arguments, polymorphic variants,
  functors or lazy. ix still compiles with OCaml 4.14 and dune

** generators
- new mini-lex and mini-yacc, reading ocamllex's and ocamlyacc's
  files: the same tokens, the same automata

** shell, editor, builder
- new mini-mk: builds all of xix, all of principia's Plan 9, and ix
- new mini-rc, against 9base's rc
- new mini-ed, against 9base's ed, with its own regexp matcher

** database
- new mini-chidb: chidb's twin (SQLite's teaching one): B-trees byte
  for byte with chidb's files, the database machine, SQL compiled to
  it, an optimizer

** version control
- new mini-git: git9's twin on git's repositories: objects, packs,
  refs, log, clone, pull and push
- new mini-diff and mini-merge3; lib_crypto (SHA-1) and
  lib_compression (zlib)

** t-ix
- tiny-build, tiny-shell, tiny-editor, tiny-db, tiny-vcs
- tiny-assembler, tiny-c, tiny-ml, and tiny-arm to run what they make;
  tiny-pi, its CPU in a Pi 4
- tiny-cpu and tiny-machine, a CPU of our own design for teaching;
  tiny-os (v0, v6, t6, in C) and tiny-kernel (in ML) for it

** libcore
- lib_core/commons: what the programs really share
- lib_core is mini-ml's stdlib (OCaml 4.14's names, ocaml-light's
  code; Printf, Lexing and Parsing new), over goken's libc
- for mini-ml only: Unix in OCaml over Linux's system calls, small
  Fpath, Logs, Fmt, and the capabilities erased

** build infra
- dune, make test (test-goken, test-ocaml, test-chidb against the
  references), Dockerfile and GHA
- the mkfiles, mkfiles/check.sh and fixpoint.sh
- make loc (scripts/stats/loc.py)

** docs
- README.md, the website and its code map, the IX logo
- a plan, a tutorial and a related-work note for each program
- docs/plans/bugs/: the bugs found in goken, chidb, ocaml-light,
  OCaml, xix and ix, each with its reproduction
- -h for every program, by examples
