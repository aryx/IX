tiny-kernel on tiny-machine, in OCaml compiled to JavaScript. Just type:
the line shows on the console above, and Enter gives it to the shell. Once a
program draws on the screen, click the screen (a blue frame) to type in it,
key by key; click outside it to type on the console again. Try ls, then
tiny-windows: in the screen, the right button gives a menu (New,
then a rectangle swept with the left button, and a shell is in it);
tetris typed there plays with the arrows.


The machine has a wire, drawn under the console; this page is its other
end. ping asks it "are you there?"; get il.txt
fetches a text of this site; wiki plan 9 (or any words) asks
Wikipedia, through the page, and paints the article's picture on the
screen a row at a time as its packets arrive. Each dot is a packet, each
line says it; the button loses the next one, and the wire's protocol asks
for it again.
