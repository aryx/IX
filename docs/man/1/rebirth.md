\title{rebirth}{1}{apps}

# NAME
rebirth – a techno studio, in a window

# SYNOPSIS
**rebirth** \[ `skin=`*name* ] \[ `meters=off` ] \[ `size=`*n* ]

# DESCRIPTION
*Rebirth* is Propellerhead's ReBirth RB-338 (1997) small: two TB-303
bass lines, a TR-808 and a TR-909 drum machine, on one clock, through a
mixer, a distortion, a delay, a compressor and a filter whose cutoff
follows a pattern. Its sound is played as it is made.

The panel is a strip for each machine: its pattern, chosen with `<` and
`>`, its level, its mute, and its 16 steps, the one playing lit. A
click on a step edits it: on a 303 a note at the click's height, or a
rest where there was one; on a drum machine the instrument edited is
put there or taken away. Over the strips are the start and stop, the
tempo, the volume and the song, which sets the four patterns; under
them the effects.

Space
:    Starts and stops the clock.

`k`, or the button SKIN
:    The next look of the panel.

Control-Q
:    The end.

What it is given:

`skin=`*name*
:    The panel's look: `rb-338` (the first), `night`, or `plain`, the
     playground's own.

`meters=off`
:    No spectrum and no scope drawn: what a slow board needs.

`size=`*n*
:    The window *n* pixels square at first.
