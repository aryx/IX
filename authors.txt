Most of the code in this repository was written by Claude (Anthropic's
models, in Claude Code), under the direction of Yoann Padioleau, who
chose what to write, in which order and how, and reviewed each step
(see docs/history.md and docs/yoann_notes/prompt-history.md), except:
 - lib_core/libc/                      by the Plan 9 authors  (from plan9,
   via goken; copied as it is, see lib_core/libc/README.md and LICENSE),
   but for lib_core/libc/ix/, which is ix's (its vlrt.c after Plan 9's)
 - lib_core/{core,base,collections,printing,system}/
   by Xavier Leroy et al. (from ocaml 1.07, via ocaml-light, and from
   ocaml 4.14; see lib_core/README.md)
 - lib_core/commons/Set_.ml            by Xavier Leroy        (from ocaml)
 - lib_core/system/{Fpath,Logs}.ml     after Daniel Bunzli    (small
   versions of fpath and logs, for mini-ml only)
 - lib_core/system/Cap*.ml             after Yoann Padioleau  (from xix)

Note that most of the remaining code is a twin of, or is inspired by,
code written by others. ix would not exist without:
 - Plan 9, by Rob Pike, Ken Thompson, Dave Presotto, Phil Winterbottom,
   Tom Duff, Andrew Hume, Russ Cox and the others of Bell Labs:
   5a, 5l, 5c, 5i, mk, rc, ed, diff, 9pi (by Richard Miller) and libc
   are the originals of mini-asm, mini-ld, mini-cc, mini-5i, mini-mk,
   mini-rc, mini-ed, mini-diff and mini-9pi
 - Principia Softwarica, xix and goken, by Yoann Padioleau: the
   literate programs explaining Plan 9's code, their port to OCaml, and
   the Plan 9 toolchain on Linux, which gave ix its plan, its
   references to compare with, and its style
 - xv6, by Russ Cox, Frans Kaashoek and Robert Morris, and the authors
   of its Raspberry Pi ports: the original of mini-xv6, and what
   mini-qemu first booted
 - OCaml, by Xavier Leroy, Damien Doligez, Jerome Vouillon, Didier Remy
   and the others of INRIA: the language ix is written in, the compiler
   mini-ml is checked against, ocamllex and ocamlyacc for mini-lex and
   mini-yacc, and the stdlib above
 - chidb, by Borja Sotomayor (University of Chicago), and SQLite, by
   Richard Hipp: the original of mini-chidb
 - git9, by Ori Bernstein, and git, by Linus Torvalds and Junio Hamano:
   the originals of mini-git
 - QEMU, by Fabrice Bellard and its many contributors: the reference
   for mini-qemu's Raspberry Pi
 - 9base and plan9port, by Russ Cox and the suckless community: the rc
   and ed that mini-rc and mini-ed are tested against

The references to the papers and books behind each program are next to
its code, and in docs/related-work/.
