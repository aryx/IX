\title{desk}{1}{system}

# NAME
desk – the wallpaper and its icons

# SYNOPSIS
**window -desk desk**

# DESCRIPTION
*Desk* draws a wallpaper and a column of icons in a window of
\man{rio}{1}'s desk layer, under all the others. A click on an icon
selects it, and a double click opens it in a new window. The right button
stays *rio*'s menu.

Its icons are the lines of `$home/lib/desktop/desk`, or else of
`/lib/desktop/desk`, in the form \man{startmenu}{1} says, in their order;
a program not in `/bin` has no icon. An icon is drawn at twice its size.

# FILES
`/lib/desktop/desk`, `$home/lib/desktop/desk`, `/lib/desktop/icons/`
