The Oberon system, in OCaml, on the Raspberry Pi.The left key of the mouse sets the caret: what is typed goes there.The right key selects; with the left key then, the selection is deleted,with the middle key it is copied to the caret.In the bar at the left, the left key brings a line to the top,the right key goes back, the middle key goes to a place in the text.A viewer's menu, dragged with the left key, moves its top;with the middle key too, the viewer goes where the mouse is.
